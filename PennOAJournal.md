# Earth, Ocean & Climate Science Journals with No OA APC through Penn
_Last updated: Sep 15, 2026_

Penn Libraries has agreements with a number of publishers that provide APC-free open access publishing for Penn authors. This document highlights selected journals covered by these agreements in the Earth, ocean, and climate sciences. Information was collected from the [Penn Libraries Open Access Agreements guide](https://upenn.openjournalfinder.com/journals/) using the Open Journal Finder.

## Citation Impact

### Journal Impact Factor

The Journal Impact Factor is defined as all citations to the journal in the current JCR year to items published in the previous two years, divided by the total number of scholarly items published in the journal in the previous two years. Journal Impact Factor provides a functional approximation of the mean citation rate per citable item.

### 2-year citedness
The average number of citations that articles published in this journal over the past 2 years have received. 

### SCImago rank
A prestige score for the journal based on both the number of citations it receives and the importance of the journals those citations come from.
